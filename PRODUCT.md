## Users

Owners of residences and businesses in Florianópolis and Greater Florianópolis who need engineering, architecture, documentation, assessment, regularization or property adaptation services.

## Product Purpose

SuperNova Engenharia needs to turn qualified visitors into initial consultation and quote requests, while making its range of technical services clear and trustworthy.

## Positioning

Engineering service for properties and businesses in Greater Florianópolis, coordinated by a named responsible engineer and designed to make complex property decisions easier to start.

## Operating Context

Visitors often arrive with an urgent property question: documentation, a renovation, a structural need, a valuation or a technical report. The initial site will collect direct contact requests; phone, address and social profiles are still open decisions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Present these services: property regularization, usucapião support, metal structure projects, property appraisal, interior design, reinforced-concrete structural projects, architectural plans and property expertise.
- Service area: Florianópolis and Greater Florianópolis, including São José, Palhoça and Biguaçu.
- A contact form should provide a useful enquiry path before phone, address and Instagram are available.
- The first deployment is a Vercel demonstration.

## Product Principles

- Explain technical work in direct, property-owner language.
- Let a visitor identify the relevant service quickly.
- Build credibility from verifiable professional information, not invented social proof.
- Create one clear route to begin a conversation.
